Crimson adaptations: dark symbolic text, ruby accent fallbacks, native red
folder variants with restrained glass gradients; original faceted app plates
and custom glyphs. Explicit alternate folder colors, warnings, errors, battery
levels and positive status colors retain their meaning. The custom artwork
is described in CUSTOM-ART-CREDITS.md and SUPPLEMENTAL-ART-CREDITS.md.

This combined theme uses no WhiteSur, Mkos or other macOS icon sources.
All copied assets and aliases are contained in this theme. Breeze-dark and
hicolor are the only inherited fallbacks. Source SVGs are editable.

The build replaces no system icon packages. The previous CrimsonGlass and
WhiteSur packs are kept inactive so the saved icon settings can be restored.
