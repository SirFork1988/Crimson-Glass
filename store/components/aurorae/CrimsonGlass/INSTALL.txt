Crimson Glass Window Decorations 1.1.0

Extract CrimsonGlass into ~/.local/share/aurorae/themes and select it in System Settings > Colors & Themes > Window Decorations.

Project: https://github.com/SirFork1988/Crimson-Glass
Full installer and optional runtime: https://github.com/SirFork1988/Crimson-Glass/releases/tag/v1.1.0
This native component contains no automatic installer or post-install hooks.
